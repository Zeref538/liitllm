# Tagalog lexicon for the code-switch scorer. One word per line, # starts a comment.
#
# Deliberately weighted toward FUNCTION WORDS (markers, pronouns, particles,
# conjunctions) rather than a broad dictionary. Function words are a closed
# class: a writer cannot produce Tagalog syntax without them, they are the most
# frequent tokens in any real sentence, and they are almost never borrowed into
# English. That makes them high-precision evidence of "this document is anchored
# in Tagalog grammar" — which is exactly the question the filter asks, and it is
# why a short curated list beats a large scraped dictionary here.
#
# Common roots follow, because a document can be Tagalog-anchored while most of
# its content words are English (that is what Taglish IS), and we still want a
# usable signal from the Tagalog side.
